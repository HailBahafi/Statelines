Redrawn figures. Every redrawn figure stands on a plate with real depth, and each state now changes form as well as colour: something is delivered, lifted, opened, dropped, stuck or slides off. Success and error no longer draw a tick or cross; the green or red tone together with that change of form carries the verdict, so `marks` is still accepted but has nothing to hide in a redrawn figure. Fingerprint is drawn whole on a reader's glass, and Receipt is now a printer feeding a slip that is stamped paid. Approval no longer throws when it enters error, which used to stop every figure on the page.
