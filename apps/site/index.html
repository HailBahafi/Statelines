Figures for the moments every app has.

Uploading, paying, typing a code, waiting on an AI reply. statelines is 101 isometric line figures that show what your app is doing, driven by one state prop.

Each figure is loading. Hover one to watch it finish, then succeed, then fail.

One prop that means something.

Wire state to your app's real status, and the figure tells the story.

Five states

idle, loading, success, error and empty. Each one has a clear picture, and colour appears only for success and error.

Light and dependency-free

No runtime dependencies. All 101 figures together are about 70 KB gzipped, under 1 KB each on average, and tree-shaking keeps only what you import.

Idle means idle

Only loading loops. Every figure shares one animation frame that sleeps once the figures settle, so a page full of them costs almost nothing.
