# The ten rules

Every figure follows these. They are what make thirty figures look like one family.

1. **One projection.** Isometric, 30°, the camera never moves. World x runs to the lower right, y to the lower left, z up.
2. **One stroke weight.** Everything is drawn at `--statelines-stroke`. A bold line (twice the weight) is reserved for progress and handles.
3. **Plates hide.** Solids are filled with the background colour so what is behind them disappears. Draw back to front.
4. **One crease.** A box shows its silhouette in its tone and a single dim crease inside. No hatching, no shading.
5. **Colour means a verdict.** Green is success and red is error, and nothing else is ever coloured. "Acting right now" is the darkest grey, not a colour.
6. **No words inside the figure.** Lines stand in for text. Captions belong outside.
7. **Rest is a picture.** The idle pose is drawn on purpose and holds still. A figure that only reads while moving is not finished.
8. **Only loading loops.** Every other state settles and stops, so a page of figures costs nothing.
9. **Empty is dashed.** What is absent is drawn as a ghost: a dashed outline in the mid grey.
10. **The pointer is a bonus.** A figure answers the pointer with one gesture, never two, and works fully without it.
